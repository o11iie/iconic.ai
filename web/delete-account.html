Delete your Slate account

App: Slate (ai.iconic.slate) · Developer: {{legalEntity}}

You can delete your Slate account and its data here, from any browser, without installing the app. You can also do it inside the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account. Both routes do exactly the same thing, immediately.

What is deleted

Deleted immediately and permanently, with no way to recover them:

  • Your account, email address and password
  • Your handle, display name and profile image
  • All signed-in sessions
  • Everything you follow, and your entire watchlist
  • Your saved watch and play journeys
  • Your favourite genres and spoiler settings
  • Your notification history and preferences
  • Your Ask Slate conversations and message history
  • Your reactions on other people's posts
  • Your Slate Pro entitlement record

What is anonymised instead of destroyed

Slate's community is threaded. If we deleted your posts outright, we would also delete replies written by people who never asked for anything to be deleted. So instead:

  • Your posts and comments: the text you wrote is removed and replaced with [deleted], and authorship is reassigned to an anonymous account. Your words and your name are gone; the surrounding conversation survives.
  • Reports you filed: kept so moderators can still act on them, but no longer linked to you.
  • Product analytics events: permanently detached from your account and kept only as anonymous counts.

How long it takes

Immediately. The deletion runs as a single database transaction while you wait — there is no queue and no cooling-off period. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before those backups rotate out; it is never restored to the live service.

Important: this does not cancel Slate Pro

Slate Pro is billed by Google Play, not by Slate, and only Google can cancel it. If you subscribe, cancel it in the Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions as well, or you will keep being charged after your account is gone.

Delete your account now

Sign in with the account you want to delete. Your password is used only to prove the account is yours and is never stored by this page.

If you cannot sign in

Email from the address on the account and ask for it to be deleted. We will verify the request and delete the account.
