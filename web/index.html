Slate

The home of entertainment hype — movies, TV and games in one place.

Slate is an Android app for people who follow what is coming next. Follow a title and Slate keeps its release date, countdown and hype signal in front of you. Build a watchlist across all three media types, plan a watch or play journey through a franchise, ask questions about what you are tracking, and talk about it with other people who are waiting too.

Data sources

Slate does not invent release dates, ratings or descriptions. Movie and TV information comes from TMDB; game information comes from IGDB. Where a date is not yet precise — a quarter, a month, or a year with no day — Slate shows exactly that rather than guessing a day.
