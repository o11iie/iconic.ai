Community Guidelines

Effective {{effectiveDate}}

Slate exists for people who are excited about what is coming next. These rules keep it that way. They apply to posts, comments, reactions, handles and display names.

Mark your spoilers

This is the rule people care about most, so Slate builds it in. When you post, mark it as containing spoilers and Slate hides it behind a tap for anyone whose spoiler setting asks for that. Deliberately posting an unmarked spoiler — especially in a title's discussion before release — gets the content removed. Do not put spoilers in a post's first line to get around the gate.

Not allowed

  • Harassment and hate. No attacks on people, and nothing that demeans anyone based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, disease, age, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, or immigration status.
  • Threats and violence. No threatening, inciting or glorifying violence against anyone, including creators, cast, developers and other users.
  • Sexual content. Slate is a general-audience app. No pornography or sexually explicit material. Absolutely no sexual content involving minors — we report it to the authorities and ban permanently.
  • Private information. Do not post anyone's address, phone number, private messages, or other personal details.
  • Impersonation. Do not pose as a studio, publisher, journalist, creator or another user.
  • Spam and manipulation. No repetitive posting, engagement farming, bots, referral spam, or vote manipulation.
  • Piracy. No links to, or requests for, unlicensed copies, streams, ROMs, cracks or leaked builds.
  • Leaks of unreleased material. No stolen scripts, unreleased footage, datamined builds or material obtained through a breach.
  • Deliberate misinformation. Do not present invented release dates, castings or announcements as fact. Clearly label a prediction or a theory as one — Slate has post types for exactly that.
  • Illegal activity. Nothing that facilitates it.

Reporting

Every post and comment has a report action. Choose the reason — spam, harassment, unmarked spoiler, misinformation, or other — and add detail. Reports go to a moderation queue that Slate's moderators review. You will not see who reported what, and the person you reported is not told who reported them.

What we do about it

Depending on how serious and how repeated it is, we may remove the content, restrict an account from posting, or terminate it. Content involving child sexual abuse material, credible threats of violence, or coordinated harassment results in immediate termination and, where appropriate, a report to law enforcement.

If we get it wrong

Email and a human will look at it again. Say what was removed and why you think the decision was wrong.

Your own content

You can delete anything you have posted. Deleting your account removes the text of every post and comment you wrote and detaches your name from them — see Delete your account.
