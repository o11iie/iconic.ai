Support

Email and a human will read it.

Things you can do yourself, right now

  • Delete your account and data — in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account, or on this website. It happens immediately.
  • Turn notifications off — all of them or per type, in Settings.
  • Manage or cancel Slate Pro — in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. Google handles the billing, so cancellations and refunds go through Google, not us.
  • Report a post or comment — use the report action on the content itself. It reaches moderators directly.

Common questions

A release date is wrong

Slate shows what TMDB or IGDB supplies and never invents a date. If a date looks wrong, it usually changed at the source or is not yet precise. Tell us which title and we will check.

I paid for Slate Pro but do not have it

Pro is granted by Slate's servers only after Google confirms the purchase, which is usually instant but can lag. Open the app and pull to refresh, or sign out and back in. If it still has not appeared after an hour, email us with the Google Play order ID from your receipt.

I deleted my account but I am still being charged

Deleting a Slate account does not cancel a Google Play subscription — only Google can cancel it. Open the Play Store, go to Payments & subscriptions, and cancel Slate Pro. For a refund on charges already taken, contact Google Play support.

I forgot my password

Email from the address on the account.

Something was removed and I think that was wrong

Email us with what was removed and why. See the Community Guidelines for what the rules are.

Reporting a security issue

Email with the details and steps to reproduce. Please give us a reasonable chance to fix it before disclosing publicly. Do not access or modify other people's data while testing.

Privacy requests

To access, correct or export your data, email from the address on your account. See the Privacy Policy for what we hold and for how long.
