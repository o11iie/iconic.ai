Where Slate's information comes from

Slate does not author entertainment metadata and does not copy provider catalogues into its own database. Titles, release dates, descriptions, artwork and ratings are fetched live from the providers below and cached only briefly.

TMDB — movies and TV

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Movie and TV information and images are supplied by The Movie Database and remain the property of their respective owners.

IGDB — games

Game information is supplied by IGDB, accessed through Twitch's API. Slate is not endorsed or certified by IGDB or Twitch. Game artwork, titles and descriptions remain the property of their respective publishers and developers.

Posters, key art and trailers

Artwork and trailer links are served from the providers and belong to the studios, publishers and rights holders that produced them. Slate displays them to identify the work being discussed and claims no ownership of them.

Release dates

Slate shows release dates at the precision the provider actually supplies — a full date, a month, a quarter, or a year. Where the provider does not give a time of day, Slate does not invent one. If a countdown looks wrong, it is because the underlying date changed or is imprecise, not because Slate estimated it.

Copyright complaints

If you believe material on Slate infringes your copyright, send a notice to including:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work you say has been infringed.
  2. Identification of the material on Slate you want removed, with enough detail for us to find it — the post or comment, and the title it appears under.
  3. Your name, address, telephone number and email address.
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on their behalf.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

We review valid notices and remove infringing material. If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, send a counter-notice to the same address with the same contact details, identification of the removed material, and a statement under penalty of perjury that it was removed by mistake or misidentification.

We terminate the accounts of users who repeatedly infringe.
